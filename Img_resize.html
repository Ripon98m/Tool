About Our Free Image Resizer Tool

Our online image resizer lets you quickly resize JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, and BMP images without losing quality. Whether you need to resize an image for Instagram, YouTube thumbnail, website, or email — our tool has presets for every occasion. You can also set custom dimensions and lock the aspect ratio to prevent distortion.

Unlike most online tools, all processing happens directly in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. This means your images never leave your device — giving you complete privacy and lightning-fast speed. No uploads, no waiting, no watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer really free? +
Yes! Our image resizer is completely free with no limits, no signup, and no watermarks added to your images.
Are my images uploaded to a server? +
No. All image processing happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your images never leave your device, ensuring 100% privacy and security.

We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, and BMP input formats. You can output in JPG, PNG, or WEBP.

Not if you use the right settings. We recommend 85-95% quality for JPG/WEBP output. Our quality slider gives you full control over the balance between file size and visual quality.
Can I resize images on mobile? +
Absolutely! Our tool is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.
